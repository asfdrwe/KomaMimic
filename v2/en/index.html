KomaMimic v2 Guide

This guide covers v2.0.0 of the latest v2 series, which adds video playback.

日本語 | English

Screenshots on this page show the Japanese UI.

KomaMimic is a comic and image viewer for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Choose folders and archives from a cover list and read single pages, spreads, or full screen. It also supports PDF, TXT, Markdown, EPUB, and JavaScript image readers and filters. KomaMimic is an independent implementation inspired by MangaMeeya; v2 extends its image and document viewing features with local video playback.

Using KomaMimic

  • Installation: install KomaMimic.
  • Basic controls: opening, navigation, lists, full screen, keyboard, and mouse.
  • Display and image processing: spreads, zoom, the bottom toolbar, processing, and saving.
  • Reading documents: TXT, Markdown, EPUB, display settings, and reading positions.
  • Playing videos: local video controls and preferences.
  • Bookshelves, history, and bookmarks: registering books, resuming, sorting, and playback.
  • Settings, reading data, and caches: storage locations, backups, and capacity.
